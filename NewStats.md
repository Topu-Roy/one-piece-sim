## Haki

### General rule for haki stats distribution

| Tier       | Haki Level            | Numeric Range | Meaning                    |
| ---------- | --------------------- | ------------: | -------------------------- |
| `none`     | No Haki               |             0 | Cannot use Haki            |
| `basic`    | Basic mastery         |       100–299 | Standard trained Haki      |
| `advanced` | Advanced mastery      |       300–499 | High-level Haki techniques |
| `supreme`  | Exceptional/god-level |       500–700 | The absolute highest level |

### What stats haki affects

| Haki        | Core Stat | Secondary | Tertiary | Core Effect | Secondary Effect | Tertiary Effect |
| ----------- | --------- | --------- | -------- | ----------: | ---------------: | --------------: |
| Armament    | Defense   | Strength  | Stamina  |        100% |              50% |             20% |
| Observation | Awareness | Speed     | BattleIQ |        100% |              40% |             30% |
| Conqueror   | Strength  | Stamina   | Defense  |        100% |              50% |             40% |

## Devil Fruit

Each DF is different so no fixed range of stats. It will vary based on the DF itself. Needs to carefully assign each stats based on it's ability.

### General idea of what each type tense to do

- Paramecia → Attack-focused
- Zoan → Balanced physical boost
- Ancient Zoan → Physical powerhouse + stamina
- Mythical Zoan → Overall strongest DF stat coverage
- Logia → Defense + speed + evasion/awareness

## Weapons

Each weapon is different so no fixed range of stats. The stat will depend on it's rarity, significance, and reliability. Needs to carefully assign each stats based on it's ability.

### General idea

- Sword = Better Strength, Speed
- Spear = Better Strength, Defense
- Club = Better Strength, Defense, worse Speed
- Rifle/Gun = Better Strength, Awareness
- Mechanical/Cyborg Weapon = Better Strength, Defense, worse Speed

### Tires of weapon and their stat distribution

| Weapon Tier | Stat Range | Meaning                   |
| ----------- | ---------: | ------------------------- |
| Basic       |     50–150 | Normal weapons            |
| Legendary   |    150–300 | Famous/powerful weapons   |
| God Tier    |    300–600 | Absolute top-tier weapons |
